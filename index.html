Good questions

Before you get started.

What devices can run QRK Board?+

QRK Board is designed for Android TV, Google TV, Amazon Fire TV and Android tablets. Check the setup instructions for the requirements and availability of each platform.

Is QRK Board really free?+

The everyday home dashboard features are free. Cameras (camera views and alerts) and Pro (custom branding and API-powered metrics) are separate monthly subscriptions.

Do I need to know how to code?+

No. You can arrange dashboard tiles with the drag-and-drop layout editor. Connecting custom data sources may require API details and some technical knowledge.

Which cameras and data sources are supported?+

Cameras is designed to support compatible Reolink camera feeds and webhook-triggered alerts; Pro adds metrics from REST APIs, GraphQL and InfluxDB. Compatibility depends on the camera, endpoint and configuration.

Does QRK Board collect personal data?+

No. QRK Board has no account, sign-in or analytics, and nothing you set up is sent to us. Your settings stay on your device, and the board connects directly to the services behind the tiles you use. The details are in the privacy policy.
