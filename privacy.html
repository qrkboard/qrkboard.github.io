Privacy

Privacy

QRK Board shows your information on your screen. It doesn't send it to us.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account, no sign-in and no analytics, and it doesn't report usage to us.

What stays on your device

Your layouts, settings, photos and the sources you add are stored on the device that runs the board (a Raspberry Pi, an Android TV or Fire TV, or a tablet). Reset in the app removes them.

What the board fetches

The board connects straight from your device to the services behind the tiles you use:

  • Weather and place search
  • News feeds you pick
  • Calendar links you paste
  • Cameras, devices and data sources you set up (REST, GraphQL, InfluxDB)

Those services see your device's address and what it asks for, under their own privacy terms. Only tiles that are on screen are fetched.

This website

It's hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps its own server logs. The site has no cookies, trackers or analytics. Fonts load from Google Fonts. If you send feedback, what you type becomes a public GitHub issue, with no name or email attached.
